Two free spirits, one beautiful adventure.

It was a warm Saturday at an open-air market in BGC when Gabriel spotted Sofia laughing beside a wildflower vendor. He bought her the whole bouquet. She told him it was cheesy. He said he'd do it again tomorrow.

Three years of road trips, late-night stargazing, slow mornings and one barefoot proposal on a Siargao beach later — here we are, ready to celebrate the wildest, most beautiful chapter of our lives with every soul we love.
